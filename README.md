# Mann Patel

Physics and applied mathematics at UC San Diego.

I build quant research backtests, physics simulations and data analysis, and Formula 1 data projects.

## Projects

- [Pairs-Trading-Walkforward](https://github.com/mann-patel07/Pairs-Trading-Walkforward): does cointegration predict profitable pairs trading? 30 US large-cap pairs and 19 walk-forward windows give a net Sharpe ratio of 0.32, with an interval from -0.36 to 0.99.
- [Trend-Following-Backtest](https://github.com/mann-patel07/Trend-Following-Backtest): a moving average crossover on 29 tickers and 19 parameter pairs. Buy-and-hold beats it in about 93% of the cells.
- [Ising-Monte-Carlo](https://github.com/mann-patel07/Ising-Monte-Carlo): the 2D Ising model with Metropolis and Wolff updates. The critical temperature is 2.2666 (standard error 0.0035) against the exact 2.2692.
- [Ising-Chain-Entanglement](https://github.com/mann-patel07/Ising-Chain-Entanglement): the entanglement entropy of the quantum Ising chain's ground state, from exact diagonalization and free fermions. The central charge comes out at 0.5007 against the exact 1/2.
- [Z-Boson-Dimuon](https://github.com/mann-patel07/Z-Boson-Dimuon): an unbinned fit of the Z mass in CMS open dimuon data. It gives 90.79 GeV, 0.40 GeV below the PDG value, a gap larger than the total uncertainty.
- [F1-Tyre-Degradation-Analysis](https://github.com/mann-patel07/F1-Tyre-Degradation-Analysis): does a tyre degradation term improve lap-time forecasts? A 2025 study planned in advance, replicated on 2022 to 2024. Pooled forecast error falls from 0.780 s to 0.640 s.
- [F1-Race-Replay-Dashboard](https://github.com/mann-patel07/F1-Race-Replay-Dashboard): an offline replay page with 431 replays (401 races and 30 sprints) from 2007 to 2026. I have stopped working on it.
- [Neutrino-waveform-Classification](https://github.com/mann-patel07/Neutrino-waveform-Classification): PyTorch models for particle-detector waveforms. The 1D CNN classifier has a ROC AUC of 0.857.
